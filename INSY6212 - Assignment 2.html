Scenario

City of Johannesburg Smart Service Portal (SSP) Project

The City of Johannesburg Metropolitan Municipality has experienced increasing complaints from residents regarding poor service delivery, slow response times to municipal issues, and lack of communication on service requests such as water leaks, electricity faults, refuse collection, and billing queries. Currently, service requests are logged through multiple disconnected channels, including call centres, walk-in offices, emails, and paper-based forms. This fragmented system has resulted in lost requests, duplicated work, poor tracking, and limited performance visibility. To improve service delivery and enhance citizen engagement, the municipality has approved the development of a Smart Service Portal (SSP) — a digital platform that will allow residents to log, track, and receive updates on municipal service requests through a web and mobile application.

The SSP will provide:

·         Online logging and tracking of service requests

·         Automated routing of requests to relevant departments

·         SMS and email notifications to residents

·         Integration with the municipal billing and customer database

·         Real-time dashboards and analytics for management

·         Geo-location features for fault reporting

·         Secure user authentication

Due to increasing public pressure and oversight from the Gauteng Provincial Government, the project must be completed within 8 months. You have been appointed as the IT Project Management Team responsible for planning, scheduling, and controlling the SSP project using recognised project management principles and appropriate tools (e.g., Microsoft Project or similar)
